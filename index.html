3. Detailed Data Collection

To provide a robust family finance ecosystem, we collect data across several vectors:

  • A. Account & Authentication Data: Facilitated via Google Firebase Authentication. We collect your Email Address, Display Name, and generate a unique User ID (UID).
  • B. Financial & Transactional Data: Inputted voluntarily by you. This includes income figures, expense categories, transaction timestamps, and budget goals, strictly stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • C. Technical & Diagnostic Data: Device model, Operating System version, App usage statistics, and crash logs to maintain our 99.9% crash-free rate.

4. The Document Vault (Strict File Handling)

Parivar Bachat provides a premium "Vault" feature allowing families to upload sensitive documents (such as ID proofs, medical receipts, and PDF reports). We handle this with the utmost security:

  • Encryption: All files uploaded via the Vault are encrypted in transit (using HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest on Google Firebase Storage servers.
  • Strict Rulesets: We utilize advanced Firebase Security Rules. Documents are cryptographically tied to your specific 'Family ID'. No other user, and no unauthorized personnel, can read or download your files.
  • Zero Profiling: We explicitly declare that we DO NOT use Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to scan your documents for marketing, profiling, or advertising purposes.

6. Data Deletion Rights (Mandatory Disclosure)

In accordance with global privacy laws and Google Play's strict Data Safety Policy, you have absolute control over your digital footprint.

Method 1: In-App Deletion

Navigate to Settings > Profile > Delete Account. This triggers a cloud function that instantly wipes your Firestore records, Auth identity, and Vault documents.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app but wish to purge your data, please fill out our secure Data Erasure Form. We process these requests within 72 hours.
